Station directory: uk-railway-stations

The accompanying stations.csv is an unmodified copy of the CSV from
https://github.com/davwheat/uk-railway-stations, commit
67dff87f51bdb782ddfcce1d8b10a88f059bf380. The publisher credits davwheat,
Trainline EU, and the Trainline sources: OpenStreetMap, SNCF OpenData,
GeoNames, Digitraffic.fi, OpenTransportData.swiss, and admin.ch.
Source details: https://github.com/trainline-eu/stations#licence

The database is available under the Open Database License 1.0 (ODbL):
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
A copy of that licence accompanies the CSV as ODbL.txt.
The directory covers Great Britain National Rail stations, not Northern Ireland.
