# About Life in UK and our editorial policy

**Search description** — How Life in UK sources, checks, translates and corrects practical Guides for people living in the UK.

Life in UK helps people find and understand practical information about living in the UK. The core Guides are free to read. They are available in Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Urdu and Hindi. We are independent of the UK government, the NHS and the providers we discuss.

## How we choose sources

We start with legislation, official guidance, regulators and public services such as GOV.UK and the NHS. We use a provider's own published terms for claims about its services. We identify the part of the UK a rule applies to and separate current rules from proposals. Where possible, a Guide links to the source and shows when it was checked. Check the official source again if you are about to act on a deadline, fee or eligibility rule.

## How we prepare Guides

We use AI to assist research, drafting and translation. We compare material claims with the cited sources, check that key rules and numbers agree across languages, and use automated checks on the Guide files. The site owner approves publication. Examples can be adapted to readers' language needs. We keep the applicable rule and its limits consistent across versions.

## Corrections

When a public corrections contact is available, we will list it here. To report an error, include the Guide link, language, the passage in question and an official source if you have one. Please leave out personal medical, immigration and financial documents or account details. We check the claim against the source and record a revised version and its date when a material correction is made.

## Independence and limitations

Our Guides provide general information. A reference to a provider does not imply endorsement. Use the appropriate official service or a qualified professional for advice about your circumstances. A Guide's publication does not certify professional review or independent review by a fluent human reader. We describe such review only when it has actually taken place and can be verified.
